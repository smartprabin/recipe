Privacy Policy

Shree Swasthani Brata Katha (श्री स्वस्थानी व्रतकथा) · Android app np.swasthani.bratakatha
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Summary

This app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no account and no sign-in, and it does not need an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

To give you a better reading experience, the app saves the following only on your own phone:

  • the last page you read and your reading progress,
  • pages you bookmark,
  • the dates on which you opened the book (to show "days read"),
  • your reading preferences (theme, brightness, warmth, layout, vibration).

This information never leaves your device. The app developer cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. The book is included inside the app.

Third parties

The app does not include advertising or analytics services and does not share data with any third party.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

सारांश

यो एपले तपाईंको कुनै पनि व्यक्तिगत जानकारी सङ्कलन गर्दैन, कुनै सर्भरमा पठाउँदैन, अरूलाई दिँदैन वा बेच्दैन। यसमा विज्ञापन, एनालिटिक्स, खाता वा साइन-इन छैन, र इन्टरनेट पनि चाहिँदैन।

तपाईंको फोनमा मात्र राखिने जानकारी

तपाईंले अन्तिम पटक पढेको पृष्ठ, चिनो राखिएका पृष्ठ, पढेका दिनहरू र पढ्ने सेटिङ (रङ, उज्यालो आदि) तपाईंकै फोनमा मात्र राखिन्छ। यो जानकारी फोनबाहिर जाँदैन र एप हटाउँदा मेटिन्छ।
