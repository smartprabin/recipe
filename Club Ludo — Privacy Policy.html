Club Ludo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Club Ludo ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Club Ludo mobile application (the "App") and how it is used. Club Ludo is an offline dice board game — gameplay itself requires no internet connection and does not involve real-money betting or wagering.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information (such as your name, email, or phone number) to play Club Ludo. The information collected comes only from third-party services used within the App, described below:

  • Advertising data: The App displays banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), IP address, general device information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Analytics and crash data: We use analytics and crash-reporting tools (such as Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics) to understand app performance and fix bugs. This may include device model, operating system version, app usage patterns, and crash logs. This data is anonymized/aggregated and is not linked to your identity.

The App does not request any special device permissions (such as camera, contacts, location, or storage access).

How We Use Information

  • To display and personalize advertisements
  • To measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • To monitor app stability, fix crashes, and improve gameplay
  • To understand overall usage trends (e.g., which features are popular)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Club Ludo uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Firebase (Analytics/Crashlytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We encourage you to review these policies to understand how these providers handle data.

Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in Android Settings > Google > Ads).
  • You can reset your Advertising ID at any time via your device settings.
  • Since gameplay itself is offline and requires no account, uninstalling the App removes all locally stored game data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Club Ludo is a family-friendly game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the App is configured for a child-directed audience, ad services are limited to those compliant with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address it.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated "Effective date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
